# TidyShot screen recording R3: design (2026-10-24)

R3 adds click highlights and a webcam bubble to the screen recording built in R1 and R2. R1's design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-tidyshot-screen-recording-design.md` and R2's is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-tidyshot-screen-recording-r2-design.md`; everything there still holds unless this document changes it. R2's decisions stand (R2, Decided 2026-10-23 and Decided 2026-10-24, 1 to 11). The research behind this design is in `.superpowers/sdd/r3/` (`research-code.md`, `research-camera.md`, `research-clicks.md`); its probe sources are in `docs/superpowers/research/recording-probes/` and none has been run yet (Probes). There is no keystroke overlay in R3.

**Tim's decisions (2026-10-24, `.superpowers/sdd/r3/decisions.md`).**
- **Features.** Click highlights and a webcam bubble. No keystroke overlay.
- **Bubble placement.** A round window on the screen that the recording films. It can be dragged anywhere, before or during recording. It starts in the recorded area's bottom-right corner, and its last place is remembered.
- **Bubble size.** A setting with Small, Medium and Large; Medium is the default. A double-click on the bubble steps through the sizes during recording.
- **Click highlights.** Left and right clicks, a short ring at each click, a different colour for right clicks. A drag keeps its ring until the button is released.
- **Controls.** Toggles in Settings → Capture → Recording, both off by default, plus a button on the recording bar for each that turns it on or off mid-recording.

Every choice below that these answers do not settle is marked **(the design's call)**, and the list at the end collects them so Tim can overrule any one.

## Probes (sources in `docs/superpowers/research/recording-probes/`, none run yet)

Each probe is Tim's to run: four of the five record the screen or open the camera, and the fifth needs his cameras plugged in. The build order in Gates says which tasks wait on which. Build the camera probes with `xcrun swiftc -O -target arm64-apple-macos26.0 -o /tmp/<name> <name>.swift -Xlinker -sectcreate -Xlinker __TEXT -Xlinker __info_plist -Xlinker r3-camera-info.plist` (the plist sets `NSCameraUsageDescription`, `NSCameraUseContinuityCameraDeviceType` and a bundle id); `r3-clicks.swift` builds without the plist. Results go in this section as R2's did, with the date and the Mac.

- **P1, the global monitor** (`r3-clicks.swift`, `monitor 60`; records nothing). Click and right-click in Finder, on the menu bar, inside an open menu, on the Dock, in Control Center, in a fullscreen app, on the 5K display and on a TidyShot window. Expected: every click prints but those on TidyShot's windows, with a delay under 20 ms; `cocoa`, `cg` and `cgEvent` agree on both displays; `AXIsProcessTrusted` and `CGPreflightListenEventAccess` print false while the events still arrive. `ring 60` then draws the overlay live, to check it is click-through and sits over menus and fullscreen apps.

- **P4, the monitor in the sandbox.** The same global monitor in a Debug TidyShot build behind a launch argument (`-ClickMonitorSpike`, as R1's `-RecordingSpike`). Expected: no prompt, and clicks on other apps arrive under App Sandbox.
- **B1, the bubble in the filter** (`r3-bubble-filter.swift`; records frames, no file). Expected: red at the bubble and not green at the decoy at the start, after a move, after a resize, after `orderOut` and back, and for a panel made after the start only once `updateContentFilter` has run, with its time in ms. A variant with an `SCRecordingOutput` attached answers whether `updateContentFilter` keeps the output writing, as `updateConfiguration` did not in R2.

Expected: the Brio 500 as `.external` and the iPhone as `.continuityCamera`, each with its formats and frame rates, `systemPreferredCamera` and `userPreferredCamera`, and connect, disconnect and preferred-camera changes as Tim plugs the Brio in and out and brings the iPhone near.

## Filming the bubble and the rings

- **Two filmed windows.** `RecordingExclusions` gains `filmedWindows: Set<CGWindowID>`: the bubble panel and the ring overlay. In `makeFilter`, area and fullscreen recordings pass `coverWindows ∪ filmedWindows`, resolved through `OverlayWindowLookup`, as `exceptingWindows` of `SCContentFilter(display:excludingApplications: [own], exceptingWindows:)`. An excepted window of an excluded app is captured (research-camera, section 1), which is how R1 films the desktop covers. R1's "What stays out" rule now has these two exceptions beside the covers; every other TidyShot window, the bar, the frame and tool tips included, stays out.
- **Made at every start.** Both windows are created in `recordingHandOff` for every area and fullscreen recording, whatever the toggles say, after the content read and before `recorder.run`, and ordered front before `driver.start`, because the lookup reads `onScreenWindowsOnly: true` and the filter is built once. Their numbers are read beside `chrome.windowNumbers`. When a feature is off, its window is on screen and invisible: `backgroundColor = .clear`, `isOpaque = false`, `alphaValue = 1`, nothing drawn, `ignoresMouseEvents = true`. The bar buttons then only draw into them. G2 gates this. **(the design's call)** over `updateContentFilter`, which R2's `updateConfiguration` experience makes the riskier path.
- **Fallback filter** (`own` missing): `excludingWindows` lists only the chrome and the cards, so both windows show with no change.
- **Lookup misses.** If the lookup cannot find the bubble or the overlay, the start goes ahead without them, as it does for a missing cover, with the log line `Recording: <bubble|highlights> window not in shareable content, not filmed`, and that feature's bar button is disabled with the tool tip `Not available for this recording`. Nothing posts.
- **Window recordings** film one window (`desktopIndependentWindow`), so neither feature can appear. No bubble and no overlay are made, the camera is not opened, the monitors are not installed, and the bar has neither button (Bar). Settings say so (Settings). **(the design's call)**: no switch to `includingWindows`, which would lose what R1 relies on `desktopIndependentWindow` for.
- **GIF** recordings get both in the MP4's pixels, so conversion needs no change. A live bubble changes every frame, so a GIF with the bubble is larger and merges no frames; accepted, with a caption (Settings) **(the design's call)**.
- **Teardown.** `RecordingScreenChrome.close` closes both windows, stops the camera session and removes the monitors. `close` runs on every end (Stop, Cancel, errors, the stream's end, quit), as R2 relies on, so nothing of R3 outlives a recording.

## Webcam bubble

- **Settings.** Capture → Recording gains, after `Count down before recording`:
  - `Show webcam bubble`, a toggle, off by default, stored as `recordingWebcamBubble`;
  - `Camera`, a pop-up patterned after `Microphone`: `System Default` (default), then each camera by name from `AVCaptureDevice.DiscoverySession(deviceTypes: [.builtInWideAngleCamera, .external, .continuityCamera], mediaType: .video, position: .unspecified)` (G3). There is no `Off` entry; the toggle is the switch. Stored as `recordingCamera` (`default`, or the device's `uniqueID`) and `recordingCameraName` (the `localizedName`, written at the pick and refreshed whenever the list sees it). The list refreshes when the tab appears and on `AVCaptureDevice.wasConnectedNotification` and `wasDisconnectedNotification`. A stored camera missing from the list stays selected as `<recordingCameraName> (not connected)`, or `Unknown camera (not connected)` with no name;
  - `Bubble size`, a segmented picker `Small` / `Medium` / `Large`, Medium the default, stored as `recordingBubbleSize` (`small`, `medium`, `large`; anything else reads as `medium`).
  - `Camera` and `Bubble size` are disabled while `Show webcam bubble` is off **(the design's call)**. The pure `RecordingSettingsLayout` gains `cameraRowsEnabled` (the toggle), `showsBubbleGIFCaption` (GIF and the toggle on) and `showsOverlayCaption` (either R3 toggle on). The captions are `A webcam bubble makes GIFs much larger.` under `Bubble size`, and `Click highlights and the webcam bubble apply to area and fullscreen recordings.` under the last R3 row.
- **Permission.**
  - **Entitlement and Info.plist**, in `project.yml` first, then regenerated: `com.apple.security.device.camera`; `NSCameraUsageDescription` = `TidyShot shows your camera in a bubble in screen recordings when you turn it on.`; `NSCameraUseContinuityCameraDeviceType` = true, or the iPhone reports as a built-in camera; `NSCameraReactionEffectGesturesEnabledDefault` = false, so a hand gesture does not fire balloons into a recording **(the design's call)**. Nothing else in the entitlements or the plist changes.
  - **Asking.** Turning `Show webcam bubble` on calls `AVCaptureDevice.requestAccess(for: .video)` when the status is `.notDetermined`, as picking a microphone does. The toggle stays on whatever the answer (Decided 2026-10-24, 7 carried over).
  - **Denied.** While the toggle is on and the status is `.denied` or `.restricted`, the rows show `Camera access is off for TidyShot.` with `Privacy Settings…`, opening `x-apple.systempreferences:com.apple.preference.security?Privacy_Camera`. The status is read again when the Settings window becomes key.
  - **Before the capture lock.** A start with the toggle on and status `.notDetermined` (an ad-hoc rebuild can lose the grant) prompts before `withCaptureLock`, after the microphone prompt when both are due, so no prompt is ever filmed or in a count-in. R2's `isAskingMicrophone` becomes `askingPermission: PermissionPrompt?` (`.microphone`, `.camera`), `refusesCapture(isCapturing:askingPermission:)` refuses every capture while either prompt is open, and the R2 log lines name the prompt: `… the microphone prompt is open` or `… the camera prompt is open` **(the design's call)**; everything else in R2's Microphone, Access, holds for both. A GIF start skips the microphone prompt as R2 says, but not the camera prompt, because the bubble applies to GIF.
  - **Under the lock**, beside `MicrophoneChoice.resolve`, the pure `CameraChoice.resolve(stored:devices:preferred:access:)` gives the device, or none, and a note (posted after Stop, Notices):
    - access not `.authorized`: no bubble, the toggle kept, `Recorded without the webcam bubble` / `Camera access is off for TidyShot.`;
    - `default`: `AVCaptureDevice.systemPreferredCamera`, read once at the start; the bubble does not follow later changes to it **(the design's call)**;
    - a present device: that device;
    - a missing device: the preferred camera, and `Recorded with <its name>` / `<name> is not connected.`;
    - no camera at all: no bubble, and `Recorded without the webcam bubble` / `No camera is connected.` (`<name> is not connected.` when a device was stored).
    - The result rides in `RecordingOptions` with the two toggles and the size, so a Settings change mid-recording does not reach the run.
  - **The bar's button** (Bar) is enabled only when the start resolved a camera and access was `.authorized`, so a mid-recording press can never raise the system prompt. Disabled, its tool tip is the note's body (`Camera access is off for TidyShot.` or `No camera is connected.`).
- **The window.** `WebcamBubblePanel`: an `NSPanel` with `[.borderless, .nonactivatingPanel]`, `isOpaque = false`, `backgroundColor = .clear`, `hasShadow = false` (a window shadow would be filmed as a dark halo), `hidesOnDeactivate = false`, never key or main, `level = .floating`, `collectionBehavior = [.canJoinAllSpaces, .fullScreenAuxiliary, .stationary, .ignoresCycle]`. The bar is ordered front after it, so the bar stays above it when they overlap. Its content view is layer-backed with `cornerRadius = side / 2` and `masksToBounds = true`, holding an `AVCaptureVideoPreviewLayer(session:)` with `videoGravity = .resizeAspectFill` (the 16:9 frame cropped to the circle), `contentsScale` from the window's `backingScaleFactor`, updated in `viewDidChangeBackingProperties`.
  - **Sizes**, the circle's diameter in points: Small 120, Medium 180, Large 260 **(the design's call)**. At 2× Large is 520 px, so the session runs at `sessionPreset = .hd1280x720`, 30 fps; 1080p costs USB bandwidth and decode for nothing visible.
  - **Border.** A 2 pt white stroke at 85 % opacity inside the circle's edge, drawn in a `CAShapeLayer` above the preview; no shadow, no name tag **(the design's call)**.
  - **Mirrored.** `automaticallyAdjustsVideoMirroring = false` and `isVideoMirrored = true` where supported, so the user sees a mirror and viewers see the same mirrored picture **(the design's call)**.
  - **Before the first frame** the circle is dark grey (white 0.15) with the border. **After a loss** (below) it shows `video.slash` in white at 40 % of the diameter on that grey.
- **Where it starts, and memory.** The pure `BubblePlacement.frame(recorded:size:insets:)` places the bubble inside the recorded rect (AppKit points: the area, or the display's `visibleFrame` for fullscreen) with its right and bottom edges `insets` from the rect's right and bottom edges, then clamps the whole bubble into the rect; a rect smaller than the bubble in either dimension centres it. The insets are stored as `recordingBubbleInsetRight` and `recordingBubbleInsetBottom` (16 pt each by default), written when a drag ends and when the recording ends, so the last place carries to the next recording whatever its area or display **(the design's call)**: one memory, not one per display.
- **Dragging.** The panel's `mouseDown` records the offset and `mouseDragged` moves the window, clamped so the whole bubble stays inside the recorded rect **(the design's call)**: a bubble outside the area would not be filmed, and a fullscreen bubble on the other display would not be either. `performDrag(with:)` is not used because it cannot clamp. The window is filmed where it is, so a drag changes nothing in the stream (B1 confirms a moved excepted window stays filmed). A right-click does nothing. Clicks on the bubble draw rings like any other click when highlights are on (Click highlights).
- **Double-click** (`clickCount == 2`) steps Small → Medium → Large → Small, keeping the centre, then clamps. The new size is written to `recordingBubbleSize` **(the design's call)**, so the next recording starts at it, as its place does.
- **During the count-in and the selection.** The area selection shows no bubble, because the area is not yet known. The bubble appears, with the camera running, at the start of the count-in (or at the hand-off when the count-in is off), so the user can place it before the recording starts; the count-in's digits are at `.screenSaver` level, above it. The camera session starts on a utility queue at the same moment, so its first frames usually land before the stream's.
- **The camera session.** One `AVCaptureSession` per recording with the resolved device's input and the preview layer as its only output; no audio input, no file output. The bubble's picture is filmed off the screen, so the camera never touches the `SCStream` or the `SCRecordingOutput`, and a camera failure never fails a recording.
  - **Loss mid-recording** (G4): on `AVCaptureSession.runtimeErrorNotification`, `wasInterruptedNotification`, `didStopRunningNotification`, or `wasDisconnectedNotification` for the device, the bubble shows the loss picture and keeps its place, the time is logged, and the recording goes on. On `wasConnectedNotification` for the same `uniqueID` the session is rebuilt with it and the picture returns with its first frame. The note after Stop is `Camera disconnected` / `<name> disconnected at <m:ss>. The bubble is blank from there.` (one note, for the first loss). A session that fails to start gives `Recorded without the webcam bubble` / `The camera could not be started.`
  - **Access revoked mid-recording** behaves as a loss, whatever the system does to the session.
- **Bar button mid-recording** (Bar): on starts the session (if not running) and shows the bubble at its remembered place; off stops the session and makes the bubble invisible and click-through, its window kept on screen for the filter. The controller owns `showsBubble`, as it owns `isMuted`.
- **Logged**: `Recording: camera <name>, <width>×<height> at <fps> fps, started in <ms> ms`, `Recording: webcam bubble on|off at <m:ss>`, `Recording: bubble size <small|medium|large>`, `Recording: camera lost at <m:ss>: <notification>`, `Recording: camera back at <m:ss>`. Never the bubble's position.

## Click highlights

- **Setting.** `Highlight clicks`, a toggle after `Count down before recording` and before `Show webcam bubble`, off by default, stored as `recordingClickHighlights`. It needs no permission and no entitlement.
- **Seeing clicks.** `NSEvent.addGlobalMonitorForEvents(matching:)` for `.leftMouseDown, .leftMouseUp, .leftMouseDragged, .rightMouseDown, .rightMouseUp, .rightMouseDragged`, plus `addLocalMonitorForEvents` with the same mask so clicks on the bubble (a TidyShot window that is filmed) draw rings too; a local event on any other TidyShot window (the bar) draws nothing. The monitors are installed at `.recordingStarted` when the toggle is on, or when the bar button turns highlights on, and removed when highlights are turned off and in `close`. No event tap, no Accessibility or Input Monitoring grant (G1). A control-click is `leftMouseDown` with `.control` and gets the right-click colour, as apps treat it.
- **The overlay.** `ClickRingOverlay`: one borderless `NSPanel` covering the recorded display only, `ignoresMouseEvents = true`, `isOpaque = false`, clear background, no shadow, `.nonactivatingPanel`, never key or main, `level = .screenSaver` (set after `isFloatingPanel`, as `SelfTimerPanel` does), so rings draw over menus, the menu bar and the bubble; `collectionBehavior = [.canJoinAllSpaces, .fullScreenAuxiliary, .stationary, .ignoresCycle]`; `sharingType` left at its default. Rings are `CAShapeLayer`s with `contentsScale` from the screen; vector paths need nothing more for Retina. It holds no layers while highlights are off.
- **The ring.** 40 pt across: a 3.5 pt coloured stroke over a 6 pt dark stroke (black at 60 %), and a 20 % fill of the colour. Left: yellow, sRGB (1.0, 0.8, 0.0). Right, and control-click: blue, sRGB (0.04, 0.52, 1.0). Yellow against blue stays apart under the common colour-vision deficiencies; no shape cue **(the design's call)**. A fullscreen GIF of a 2560 pt display at 1280 px draws it 20 px across with a 1.75 px stroke and a 3 px edge, about the smallest that reads (P3 checks).
- **Animation**, from the pure `ClickRingTiming`:
  - on down: grow from 0.5× to 1× over 0.15 s, fully opaque;
  - while down: hold at 1×, and follow the pointer on each drag event, so a drag keeps its ring until release;
  - on up: once the grow has finished, expand to 1.3× and fade to clear over 0.35 s, then the layer is removed.
  - A quick click is visible for 0.5 s: about 7 frames at 15 fps and 5 at 10 fps, so no GIF rate misses it; a ring only while the button is down (about 0.1 s) would.
  - Left and right can be down together; each button has its own ring.
- **Where rings draw.** The pure `ClickHighlightRule` takes each event's CG point (`cgY = NSScreen.screens[0].frame.maxY − cocoaY`) and draws a ring only if it is inside the recorded display's frame and, for an area recording, inside the area's rect, converted to the overlay's coordinates on that display. Events outside are dropped, an up or a drag with no matching down (a button held when the recording started) is ignored, and no position is logged. On Tim's two displays only the recorded one has an overlay, so nothing shows on the other.
- **Nothing outside a recording.** The monitors exist from `.recordingStarted` to the end, never during the count-in or the selection, and the overlay only for an area or fullscreen recording's length.
- **Bar button mid-recording** (Bar): on installs the monitors; off removes them, and a ring in flight finishes its fade **(the design's call)**. The controller owns `highlightsClicks`.
- **Logged**: `Recording: click highlights on|off at <m:ss>`. Never a click's position or count.

## Bar

- **Two buttons**, between the time and the mute button, for every area and fullscreen recording, on or off, and never for a window recording **(the design's call)**:
  - **Highlights**: `cursorarrow.rays` while on, `cursorarrow` while off; tool tips `Stop highlighting clicks` / `Highlight clicks`; accessibility labels the same.
  - **Bubble**: `video.fill` while on, `video.slash.fill` while off; tool tips `Hide the webcam bubble` / `Show the webcam bubble`; accessibility labels `Hide webcam bubble` / `Show webcam bubble`. Disabled with the note's body as its tool tip when the start resolved no camera (Webcam bubble, Permission).
  - Order left to right: time, highlights, bubble, mute (or the `GIF` tag before the time), Stop, Cancel.
- **Widths.** `RecordingPanelLayout.Bar` becomes a struct (`showsTag`, `showsMute`, `showsOverlayButtons`), and the width is `172 + 36 × slots`, where the tag or the mute button is one slot and the two R3 buttons two: 172 and 208 for a window recording (as today), 244 for an area or fullscreen MP4 with no sound, 280 with sound or for GIF. Height 44, buttons 28 pt, spacing 6, leading 16, trailing 8, as today; `9:59:59` fits every bar.
- **State.** The controller owns both, as it owns mute: `toggleHighlights()` and `toggleBubble()` guard on `isRunning` and `cause == nil`, flip the value and call `chrome.setHighlights(_:)` / `chrome.setBubble(_:)`. `RecordingChrome.show` gains `onHighlights` and `onBubble`, and `RecordingChrome` gains `setHighlights`, `setBubble` and `filmedWindowNumbers`; `FakeRecordingChrome` and `FakeRecordingDriver` grow with them. A press before the first frame applies at `.recordingStarted`. A press once the recording is ending is ignored, and both buttons are disabled with Stop. The initial state is the settings snapshot; a bar press does not write Settings **(the design's call)**; the bubble's size step and place do (Webcam bubble).

## Notices and R2

- **The hold** (Decided 2026-10-24, 8) covers R3's notes as any other: nothing posts during the count-in or the recording. The camera notes post after Stop through `RecordingNoticeOrder.afterStop(save:stop:microphone:camera:quitMode:)`: the save's notification, `Recording stopped`, the microphone note, then the camera note. Quit mode posts none. At most one camera note per recording: the start's, or the loss's when the start gave none.
- **Captures blocked during a prompt** (Decided 2026-10-24, 9) covers the camera prompt through `askingPermission` (Webcam bubble, Permission).
- **Mute** is unchanged: the bubble has no sound, and the camera's microphone is never opened.
- **The microphone prompt** comes first, the camera prompt second, both before the lock; a refusal of either records without that feature.
- **Trim** cuts pixels the rings and the bubble are already part of; nothing changes.
- **GIF** gets both as pixels (Filming); the 30 s soft warning stands.
- **Recovery**: R3 writes nothing to `tmp`; a crash leaves no R3 file, and the recovered MP4 holds whatever was filmed.
- **Quit** goes through `run` returning and `recorder.close()`, which tears R3 down (Filming, Teardown). `finishRecordingForQuit` is unchanged.

## Budget and logging

- **CPU**: the bubble under 5 % of one core in TidyShot's process at Medium with 720p30 (the preview layer is fed by the capture service; the camera's own cost sits in system processes), and click highlights under 1 %. **Memory**: under 60 MB added while the bubble shows (a few 720p buffers and the layer), nothing measurable for rings. C2's numbers and the hand checks check these; over budget goes back to Tim (G5).
- **Latency**: the face lags by camera-to-screen time plus one screen frame; R2 measured the microphone 0.02 s (USB) to 0.13 s (iPhone) late, so lip sync is their difference. Measured in the hand checks with a flash-and-tone recording through the camera, as `mute-offset-mic.swift` did for sound; nothing compensates in R3.
- **Logged**: the lines under Webcam bubble and Click highlights, plus at the start `Recording: filming <n> own windows (<bubble, highlights>)`. No positions, no click counts, no frames.

## Tests (Swift Testing)

Each suite pins pure decisions; the windows, the monitors and the camera are hand checks in `docs/testing/recording-r3-manual-checklist.md`.

- `CameraChoiceTests` (pure, over a fake device list and injected access): `default`, a present device, a missing device (the preferred camera and the `Recorded with` note, the `(not connected)` label, with and without a stored name), no camera at all, access `.denied`, `.restricted`, refused and `.notDetermined` (no device, the toggle kept, the access note); a GIF start keeps the choice (unlike the microphone); stored-value parsing and the two settings written back; the pop-up's entries.

- `RecordingSettingsLayoutTests`: `cameraRowsEnabled` follows the bubble toggle; the GIF caption shows for GIF with the bubble on only; the overlay caption shows with either toggle on.

## The design's calls (for Tim to overrule)

1. Both windows are made at every area and fullscreen start and kept on screen, invisible when off, instead of `updateContentFilter` mid-recording (Filming).
2. Window recordings get neither feature and no bar buttons; no switch to `includingWindows`; P2 is informational (Filming, Bar).
3. GIFs with the bubble are accepted larger, with the caption `A webcam bubble makes GIFs much larger.` (Filming, Settings).
4. `Camera` and `Bubble size` are disabled while `Show webcam bubble` is off (Webcam bubble, Settings).
5. `NSCameraReactionEffectGesturesEnabledDefault` is false (Permission).
6. R2's `isAskingMicrophone` becomes `askingPermission` with a `.microphone` / `.camera` prompt, and the log lines name the prompt (Permission).
7. `System Default` reads `systemPreferredCamera` once at the start and does not follow it (Permission).
8. Sizes 120, 180 and 260 pt; 720p30 (The window).
9. A 2 pt white border at 85 %, no shadow, no name tag (The window).
10. Mirrored (The window).
11. The place is one memory of two insets from the recorded rect's bottom-right corner, not per display (Where it starts).
12. The drag is clamped to the recorded rect (Dragging).
13. A double-click's size step is written to the setting; a bar press is not (Double-click, Bar).
14. Yellow and blue with no shape cue (The ring).
15. Turning highlights off mid-recording lets a ring in flight finish its fade (Click highlights).
16. The two bar buttons show in every area and fullscreen bar, giving 244 and 280 pt bars (Bar).
